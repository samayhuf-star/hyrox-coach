Hybrid Trainer is a training companion for hybrid fitness: running mixed with functional strength work. Open the app, see today's session and follow it one step at a time.

TODAY, YOUR WAY
A ready session each day with duration, rounds and level shown up front. Not feeling it? Try another workout or adjust the one you have.

CLEAR GUIDANCE AT EVERY STEP
A workout player with a timer, the next step in view, and looping demos with short cues for each movement. Pause, skip or undo at any time. Demos work offline.

LEARN THE MOVEMENTS
Browse the exercise guides before you start, from brisk walks and goblet squats to sled, carry, row, ski and wall ball work.

COACH CHAT (OPTIONAL)
Ask about a movement, adapt a session or plan recovery. You get 20 free replies each month. Coach needs a sign-in with your email; the rest of the app does not. Coach is an automated assistant, not a human trainer, and not a substitute for professional care.

TRACK YOUR MOMENTUM
Levels, XP and a weekly rhythm view help you keep going.

HYBRID PLUS (OPTIONAL)
Free training stays free. Hybrid Plus adds the custom workout builder and progress insights. It is an auto-renewing subscription offered monthly or annually. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple account settings. Restore purchases is available on the plans screen.

PRIVACY
Your workouts, results and notes are stored on your iPhone. Coach messages are sent to a third-party service provider only after you consent.

Apple Health and Apple Watch tracking are not included. Sessions are general training ideas, not medical advice or personalised coaching. Not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by HYROX.
